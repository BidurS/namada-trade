    Staked

    Most NAM is staked with validators. Staked NAM can’t be sold.

    341,947,228NAM

  2. 2

    Unlocked

    Asking to unstake starts a wait of about 13.75 days.

    13.75days

  3. 3

    Bridged

    To be sold, NAM crosses to Osmosis. Crossings usually come before sales.

    19,924NAM in 7 days
